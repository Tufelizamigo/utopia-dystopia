Utopia vs Dystopia

Utopia

A perfect, ideal world where life is good for everyone.

Traits

  • ☮️ Peace
  • ⚖️ Equality
  • 🕊️ Freedom
  • 😊 Happiness

Examples

  • Star Trek

Dystopia

A broken, frightening world where everything has gone wrong.

Traits

  • 🔒 Control
  • 👁️ Surveillance
  • 😨 Fear
  • 📢 Lies

Examples

  • The Hunger Games
  • Brave New World

Utopia imagines the best world we could build. Dystopia imagines the worst world we could build.
